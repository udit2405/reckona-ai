Common questions

What is an AI rank tracker?

Unlike a Google rank tracker, there's no single position to track in an AI answer — you're either mentioned, recommended and described accurately, or you're not. An AI rank tracker runs a fixed panel of realistic buyer questions across engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude, and scores whether your brand appears and how accurately.

Why can't this tool check the AI engines automatically?

Automated, live checking requires paid API access to every engine and re-running the same panel monthly to track change over time — that's what our paid GEO Audit service does. This free tool generates your test panel and scoring guide so you can run the check yourself in minutes, no cost, no email required.

How often should I re-run my AI visibility panel?

Monthly is a reasonable cadence for a growing brand — AI engines update frequently, and a panel re-run monthly lets you catch both new citations and any hallucinations early.
