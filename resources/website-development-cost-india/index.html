What actually drives the price

  • Custom design vs template. A template-based build is cheaper but harder to make distinctive; fully custom design costs more but compounds brand value.
  • CMS and content volume. A site your team can update themselves costs more upfront than a static build, but saves on every future change.
  • Integrations. Payment gateways, CRM connections, booking systems each add real scope.
  • Performance and SEO foundations. A site built for 90+ PageSpeed and clean technical SEO from day one costs more than one bolted on later — but far less than fixing it after launch.

The cheap-quote trap: a very low quote usually means a template with no real SEO foundation, no CMS training, and hidden costs for "extra" pages later. Compare scope line-by-line, not just the headline number.
