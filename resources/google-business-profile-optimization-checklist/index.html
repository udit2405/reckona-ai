Google Business Profile optimization: the complete checklist

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

Your Google Business Profile is often the very first thing a local customer sees — before your website, before a review site. Most businesses claim it once and never touch it again. Here's the full checklist to actually optimize it.

The setup checklist

ElementWhat to get right
Primary categoryThe single most important field — choose the most specific accurate category, not the broadest
Secondary categoriesAdd every genuinely relevant one — expands which searches you appear for
Business descriptionClear, keyword-natural description of what you do and for whom
Hours & special hoursKeep current — wrong hours actively lose customers who show up at a closed door
Service area / addressAccurate to how customers actually find you

The ongoing maintenance checklist

  • Photos monthly. Real, current photos — Google favors profiles with recent visual activity.
  • Posts weekly or biweekly. Offers, updates, events — signals an active, trustworthy business.
  • Q&A monitored. Answer questions promptly, and seed the most common ones yourself before customers have to ask.
  • Reviews requested and answered. See our review automation guide for a system that doesn't feel robotic.

Mistakes that quietly hurt visibility

  • Duplicate listings for the same business (merge or remove them)
  • Keyword-stuffing the business name field (against Google's guidelines, and it can trigger a suspension)
  • Ignoring negative reviews instead of responding professionally
  • Never updating photos, making the listing look abandoned

The compounding habit: a profile updated weekly, with fresh photos and answered reviews, consistently outranks a technically "complete" profile that's been untouched for a year.
