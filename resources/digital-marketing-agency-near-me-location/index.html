'Digital marketing agency near me': does location actually matter?

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20266 min read

Searching for a local agency made sense when marketing meant print ads and local newspaper placements. For most of what a modern digital and AI-automation partner does, physical proximity matters far less than it used to — but not zero.

What genuinely benefits from being local

  • In-person workshops and training — easier to run well face-to-face for larger teams.
  • Deep local-market knowledge — hyper-local businesses (restaurants, clinics) sometimes benefit from a partner who knows the neighborhood.
  • Occasional in-person strategy sessions — valuable, but usually only need to happen a few times a year, not weekly.

What works just as well, or better, remote

WorkWhy remote is fine
SEO, GEO, content, paid adsEntirely digital work — screen-shares and dashboards, not office visits
AI automation buildsDeployed to your cloud regardless of where the team sits
Reporting and strategy callsA well-run video call beats a rushed in-person meeting

A remote-first team can also mean access to specialists you wouldn't find in every city — a genuine GEO specialist or automation engineer is still relatively rare, and geographic search narrows your options for no real benefit.

The better filter

Instead of "near me," ask: does this team communicate clearly and often, show working output weekly, and understand my specific business — regardless of city? See our fuller agency-choosing framework for the questions that actually matter.
