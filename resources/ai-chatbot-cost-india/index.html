AI chatbot cost in India: what to budget

By Reckona AIUpdated 28 July 20267 min read

Chatbot pricing spans from a free template tool to a lakh-plus custom build, and the difference usually comes down to how well it's actually grounded in your business and how much ongoing accuracy work goes into it.

Typical cost structure

TypeTypical build costOngoing monthly cost
Basic FAQ bot (template-based)₹10,000 – ₹40,000Low, mostly platform fee
Custom-trained bot on your documents₹75,000 – ₹2,50,000Model usage + maintenance
Multi-channel agent (WhatsApp + web + CRM integration)₹1,50,000 – ₹5,00,000+Higher, scales with conversation volume

These are market benchmarks — Reckona quotes every AI Automation engagement after a free audit scoped to your actual use case, not off a fixed sheet.

What actually drives the price

  • How much it needs to know. A bot grounded in your full product catalog and policy documents costs more to build correctly than a simple FAQ script.
  • Integration depth. Connecting to your CRM, inventory or booking system adds real engineering scope beyond the conversational layer itself.
  • Model usage costs. Ongoing AI model costs scale with conversation volume — a low-traffic bot costs very little to run; a high-volume one needs that factored into monthly budget.
  • Accuracy and maintenance. A bot that stays accurate as your business changes needs ongoing updates, not a one-time build.

The cheap-quote trap: a very low quote often means a generic template with no real grounding in your business — it may answer confidently and wrongly, which costs more in customer trust than the setup ever saved.
