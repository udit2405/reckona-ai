SEO for logistics and manufacturing companies in India

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

B2B logistics and manufacturing SEO looks nothing like consumer SEO — the buyer is a procurement manager comparing specs, not a consumer clicking an ad. Here's what actually works for these categories.

Why B2B industrial SEO is different

Consumer SEOLogistics / manufacturing SEO
High search volume, short buying cycleLower volume, long research and approval cycle
Emotional, brand-driven contentSpecification, capability and compliance-driven content
Local or national reachOften needs to reach international buyers and importers

What actually ranks for these categories

  • Specification and capability pages. Buyers search by exact product specs, materials, capacities and certifications — generic "about us" content doesn't capture this intent.
  • Technical foundations. Catalogue-heavy sites need clean crawlability and schema to be indexed at all — see our technical SEO checklist.
  • Export and compliance content. Certifications, standards compliance and export documentation pages capture international buyer research.
  • Case studies with real numbers. B2B buyers trust specifics — a documented capability outcome beats generic claims.
