Review management automation for local businesses

By Reckona AIUpdated 25 July 20267 min read

Reviews compound two ways: they win the map pack, and they're a corroboration signal AI engines read when deciding what to say about you. Most businesses either ignore review requests entirely or send templated messages that feel automated. Here's how to automate it without sounding like a bot.

Why reviews matter twice as much now

Reviews still drive local rankings and consumer trust directly. What's changed: AI engines also read your reviews as evidence when describing your business — a thin or old review base makes it harder for AI to confidently recommend you, even if your service is excellent.

The automated workflow

StepWhat runs automatically
TriggerReview request sent via WhatsApp/SMS shortly after a completed visit or delivery, when satisfaction is freshest
AskA short, personal-sounding message with a direct review link — not a generic blast
Follow-upOne gentle reminder if no response after a few days, then stop
ResponseEvery review gets a reply — positive ones thanked, negative ones addressed and taken offline

How to keep it from sounding robotic

  • Personalize the trigger, not just the name. Reference the actual service or product, not a generic "thanks for visiting."
  • Time it well. Right after a good experience, not days later when the moment's gone.
  • Write real reply templates in your voice, with enough variation that ten replies in a row don't read identically.

The compounding effect: steady, recent reviews outperform a large stale pile — both for the map pack and for how confidently AI engines describe you.
