WhatsApp catalog and commerce setup: a step-by-step guide

By Reckona AIUpdated 28 July 20267 min read

WhatsApp Catalog turns your Business Profile into a browsable storefront customers never have to leave the chat to explore — and most Indian small businesses either haven't set it up or have a half-finished, outdated version.

The setup checklist

StepWhat to get right
Verified WhatsApp Business accountRequired foundation before catalog features are available
Product listingsClear photos, accurate pricing, and descriptions written for quick scanning, not paragraphs
CategoriesOrganized so customers can browse, not just search by name
Quick replies & automated FAQsCommon questions (delivery time, sizes, returns) answered instantly

In-chat checkout options

Depending on your setup, customers can add items to a cart within WhatsApp and either complete payment in-chat or be routed to a payment link — removing the friction of switching to a separate app or website mid-purchase, which is where many impulse and repeat purchases are lost.

Common setup mistakes

  • Outdated catalog. Discontinued products or old prices still listed — actively damages trust when a customer discovers the mismatch.
  • No automated response for after-hours enquiries. A catalog with no reply system loses the exact impulse-buy moment it was built to capture.
  • Poor product photography. Catalog images are often the deciding factor for a WhatsApp-first buyer — treat them as seriously as website product photos.

Keep it current or don't bother. An outdated catalog is worse than none — it actively creates a bad first impression instead of just missing an opportunity.
