How to choose an SEO agency in India: a buyer's checklist

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

Every SEO agency's homepage promises the same things — proven results, custom strategy, dedicated team. Here's what to actually ask before signing, and the answers that separate real expertise from a well-designed sales pitch.

The questions that actually reveal something

Ask thisWhat a good answer sounds like
"How do you measure success in the first 90 days?"Specific leading indicators (rankings for target terms, technical fixes shipped) — not just "we'll grow your traffic"
"Can I see a real audit example?"A concrete, prioritized document — not a generic template with your domain swapped in
"Who actually does the work?"A named team, not an anonymous "our experts"
"What happens if targets aren't met?"A clear answer, not a dodge — see our own 90-day promise as one example of what accountability looks like

Red flags worth walking away from

  • Guaranteed rankings. No honest agency controls Google's algorithm enough to guarantee a specific position.
  • Vague reporting. If they can't show you what a monthly report actually looks like before you sign, expect vague reports after.
  • Ownership unclear. You should own your Search Console, Analytics and any content produced — always.
  • One-size-fits-all packages with no audit before quoting a scope.

In-house, freelancer or agency?

Each fits a different stage — see our fuller breakdown in SEO agency vs freelancer vs in-house. Broadly: freelancers suit narrow, well-defined projects; agencies suit ongoing multi-discipline programs; in-house suits large, mature teams that can sustain full-time specialists.

The single best filter: ask for a free audit before committing to anything. A real agency's audit finds specific, verifiable issues on your actual site — not generic advice that would apply to any business.
