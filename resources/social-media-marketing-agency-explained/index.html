What a social media marketing agency actually delivers

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20267 min read

"Social media management" can mean anything from a college intern scheduling posts to a full content engine with strategy, production and community management. Here's what a real engagement actually includes.

A real monthly deliverables list

DeliverableWhat it should include
Content calendarPlanned and approved a month ahead, not improvised daily
Original content productionPhotography, video, carousels — not just recycled stock graphics
Community managementComments and DMs actually monitored and responded to, not ignored
Performance reportingReach, engagement and — critically — leads or sales attributed, not just follower count

The metric that actually matters

Follower count and likes feel good but rarely pay bills directly. The metric worth tracking is whether social activity produces measurable enquiries, site visits or brand search lift — vanity metrics without that link are a red flag in a monthly report.

Platform-specific engines

  • LinkedIn — best for B2B; see our founder distribution guide.
  • Instagram — visual brands, D2C, hospitality, fitness.
  • YouTube — long-form authority and education content, strong SEO side-benefit.

The consistency test: one channel run consistently for six months usually outperforms four channels run sporadically. Ask any prospective agency how they'll ensure consistency, not just creativity.
