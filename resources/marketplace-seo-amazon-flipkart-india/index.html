Marketplace SEO for Amazon and Flipkart sellers in India

By Reckona AIUpdated 25 July 20268 min read

Marketplace search is its own ecosystem — different ranking factors, different buyer intent, and a completely different algorithm than Google. For D2C brands selling on Amazon and Flipkart, winning marketplace search is often higher-leverage than winning Google search for the same product.

How marketplace search differs from Google

Amazon's A9/A10 and Flipkart's algorithms weight conversion rate, sales velocity and reviews far more heavily than backlinks or domain authority — the classic SEO signals barely matter here. A listing that converts well climbs, regardless of your site's Google rankings.

The five levers that move marketplace rank

LeverWhat to do
Title & keywordsFront-load the primary search term; include size, variant and use-case keywords buyers actually type
Backend search termsFill hidden keyword fields with synonyms and misspellings you won't put in the visible title
Images & A+ contentHigher-quality images and enhanced content lift conversion rate, which lifts rank
Review velocityA steady flow of recent reviews outweighs a large but stale review count
Price & stock consistencyOut-of-stock periods and price volatility both hurt rank recovery

Marketplace SEO vs your own site's SEO

They're complementary, not competing. Your own site's SEO and GEO build a brand buyers search for by name and that AI engines recommend; marketplace SEO wins the sale once they're comparison-shopping on Amazon or Flipkart. Strong brand search off-platform also lifts your marketplace conversion rate, because buyers arrive already convinced.

The compounding move: off-platform content and reviews that build trust in your brand name directly lift on-platform conversion — and conversion is the strongest marketplace ranking signal there is.
