Technical SEO checklist: what to fix first

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20269 min read

Most technical SEO audits return forty items with no sense of priority — so nothing gets fixed. Here's the checklist in the order that actually moves rankings, from whether Google can even find the page down to polish.

Tier 1: crawlability and indexing (fix these first)

CheckWhy it blocks everything else
robots.txt not blocking key pagesA blocked page can't rank, no matter how good the content is
XML sitemap submitted and currentHelps crawlers discover pages faster, especially on newer sites
No orphan pagesPages with zero internal links are hard for crawlers to find at all
Canonical tags correctWrong canonicals can make Google index the wrong version, or none

Tier 2: Core Web Vitals and mobile

Once Google can find and index your pages, page experience becomes a ranking factor. Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift and Interaction to Next Paint all matter, and mobile performance matters more than desktop for most Indian traffic — see our deeper dive on website speed and conversion in India.

Tier 3: structured data

Schema markup doesn't directly boost rankings, but it helps search engines (and increasingly AI engines) understand your content correctly — Organization, Product, FAQ and LocalBusiness schema are the highest-leverage starting points. See our entity SEO guide for how this feeds AI visibility too.

Tier 4: on-page polish

  • Title tags and meta descriptions unique per page, not templated
  • Heading hierarchy logical (one H1, nested H2/H3)
  • Image alt text descriptive, not keyword-stuffed
  • Internal linking connecting related pages — see our internal linking guide

The 80/20 of technical SEO: Tier 1 issues alone can be the difference between a page ranking at all and never appearing. Fix crawlability before spending a rupee on content.
