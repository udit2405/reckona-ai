How much does SEO cost in India? A realistic breakdown

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

SEO pricing in India ranges wildly — from a few thousand rupees a month to lakhs — and most of that spread is explained by scope, not quality alone. Here's a realistic market view to help you sanity-check quotes.

Typical market ranges (India, 2026)

Business stageTypical monthly rangeWhat it usually includes
Local / small business₹15,000 – ₹40,000Local SEO, GBP management, light content
Growing SME₹40,000 – ₹1,20,000Technical SEO, content program, on-page + some off-page
Mid-market / competitive niche₹1,20,000 – ₹3,00,000+Full-stack SEO, digital PR, enterprise technical work
Enterprise (10K+ pages)Custom, scopedLog-file analysis, dedicated pod, board-level reporting

These are market benchmarks, not a rate card — actual pricing depends heavily on competition in your category, current site health, and how much content/technical work is needed. Reckona itself quotes every engagement after a free audit rather than off a fixed sheet, for exactly this reason — see our pricing approach.

What actually drives cost up or down

  • Competitiveness of your keywords. A local service business and a national SaaS company face wildly different competition, even at similar site sizes.
  • Current site health. A technically broken site needs a bigger initial investment before content work even starts paying off.
  • Content volume needed. Categories requiring deep expert content cost more to produce well than commodity topics.
  • Link building included or not. Digital PR and link building add meaningfully to scope and cost.

The question that matters more than the price: what specifically is included at that price, and what's the plan if it doesn't move the needle in 90 days? A cheap quote with vague scope usually costs more in wasted months than a clear, higher quote.
