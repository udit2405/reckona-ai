ChatGPT SEO: what it is and how it actually works

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

"ChatGPT SEO" is the practice of optimizing so that when ChatGPT browses the web or draws on its training data to answer a question, your brand is the one it names. It's a different game from Google ranking, with its own rules.

Two ways ChatGPT surfaces your brand

ModeHow it worksWhat matters
Live browsingChatGPT searches the web in real time and cites sourcesCurrent SEO signals, crawlability, freshness
Training-data recallChatGPT answers from what it learned during training, no live searchHow widely and consistently you were discussed online as of the training cutoff

These require different strategies: live-browsing visibility is closer to classic SEO (be findable, be quotable, be current), while training-data presence is slower to build and depends on sustained mentions across the web over time.

What actually improves ChatGPT SEO

  • Entity clarity. ChatGPT needs to reliably distinguish your brand from similarly named ones — see entity SEO.
  • Consistent facts everywhere. Contradictions between your site and third-party mentions create uncertainty the model resolves by hedging or guessing.
  • Being discussed, not just discoverable. Reviews, forum mentions, press coverage and directory listings all feed training-data presence over time.

The tactical checklist for winning live citations specifically — schema, quotable structure, citable data — is in how to get cited by ChatGPT. This article is the "what and why"; that one is the "how."
