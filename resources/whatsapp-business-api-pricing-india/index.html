WhatsApp Business API pricing in India explained

By Reckona AIUpdated 28 July 20267 min read

Confusion between the free WhatsApp Business app and the paid, verified Business API is one of the most common points of friction for Indian businesses trying to scale their WhatsApp presence. Here's the actual difference.

Free app vs verified API

WhatsApp Business AppWhatsApp Business API
CostFreeConversation-based pricing + platform/BSP fees
UsersOne phone, limited team accessMultiple team members, integrated with CRM/automation
AutomationBasic auto-replies onlyFull chatbot, catalog, automated flows
VerificationBusiness badge optionalGreen tick verification available for eligible businesses

How conversation-based pricing works

The API charges per 24-hour conversation window, not per message — once a conversation starts, all messages within that window are covered under one conversation charge. Categories include marketing, utility, authentication and service conversations, each priced differently. Business-initiated marketing conversations typically cost more than user-initiated service conversations.

What businesses commonly get confused about

  • Thinking the free app scales. It works for very small operations but hits a hard ceiling on team size and automation.
  • Not accounting for BSP (Business Solution Provider) fees. Most businesses access the API through a provider, which adds its own platform fee on top of Meta's conversation charges.
  • Assuming all messages cost the same. Service replies to an existing customer conversation are typically cheaper than initiating a new marketing conversation.

Budget for the BSP platform fee, not just conversation charges. The per-conversation cost from Meta is only part of the total — factor in your provider's monthly platform or per-message fee too.
