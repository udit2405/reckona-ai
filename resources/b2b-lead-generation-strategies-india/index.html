B2B lead generation strategies that actually work in India

By Reckona AIUpdated 28 July 20268 min read

B2B lead gen advice online is mostly written for US SaaS companies with different budgets and buyer behavior. Here's what actually works for Indian B2B companies — services, manufacturing, and mid-market software alike.

The channels ranked by what actually converts

ChannelWhy it works for Indian B2B
LinkedIn (founder + company)Where Indian procurement and decision-makers actually research vendors — see founder distribution
Referrals and warm introsStill the highest-converting source for most Indian B2B — worth systematizing, not just hoping for
Category and comparison SEO contentCaptures buyers already actively researching, high intent
GEO / AI visibilityIncreasingly, buyers ask AI to shortlist vendors — see our GEO practice
LinkedIn ABM adsPrecise targeting by title and company for higher-value deals

Tactics that generate activity, not revenue

  • Cold email at high volume with no personalization. Low reply rates, high spam-complaint risk.
  • Gated content nobody wants. A whitepaper behind a form filters out most real interest before it starts.
  • Trade show booths without a follow-up system. Business cards collected and never actioned.

Building one coordinated system

The businesses that actually fill a pipeline treat lead gen as one system, not disconnected tactics: content that earns organic and AI visibility, feeding a CRM with automated scoring and follow-up, with founder LinkedIn presence building trust before the first call. Fragmented, ad-hoc activity across channels rarely compounds.

The real bottleneck is usually follow-up, not lead volume. Most Indian B2B companies generate more leads than they properly work — see our AI sales agents vs human SDRs for how automation closes that gap.
