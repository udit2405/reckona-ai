SEO for multi-location businesses in India: the practical playbook

By Reckona AIUpdated 26 July 20268 min read

A five-location clinic chain or a ten-outlet retail brand can't run one generic SEO strategy — each location competes in its own local map pack, with its own competitors. Here's how to structure it so growth in one city doesn't cannibalize another.

The core structure: one page per location

Each location needs its own indexable page — not just a listing in a locations directory — with its own address, hours, local content and Google Business Profile. A single generic "our locations" page competing for every city at once loses the map pack everywhere.

ElementWhy per-location
Dedicated URLEach location page ranks independently for its own local searches
Google Business ProfileOne profile per physical location, verified and actively managed
Local contentGenuinely local details — nearby landmarks, local team, area-specific offers
LocalBusiness schemaPer-location structured data, not one block for the whole chain

Avoiding the duplicate-content trap

The common mistake: copying the same template text across every location page with only the city name swapped. Search engines discount this, and it reads as lazy to visitors too. Genuine local specificity — real photos, real local team mentions, actual local reviews — is what separates ranking location pages from ones that quietly get ignored.

Managing profiles at scale

Ten locations means ten Google Business Profiles that all need consistent categories, current hours and an active review-response cadence. At this scale, manual upkeep breaks down — this is exactly where review automation and centralized profile management earn their keep.

One brand, many local battles: corporate consistency at the brand level, genuine local specificity at the location level — both matter, and they're not the same job.
