How to optimize for Google AI Overviews

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

Google AI Overviews now answer a large share of searches directly at the top of the results page, often before a single blue link. Whether you're cited there increasingly matters more than your position below it. Here's how the feature actually works.

How AI Overviews choose sources

Unlike a classic featured snippet pulling from one page, AI Overviews synthesize across several sources and cite the ones it judges most relevant and trustworthy. It leans heavily on pages that already rank well organically, but ranking well doesn't guarantee a citation — it's a filter, not a formula.

What increases your odds

FactorWhy it helps
Direct-answer structureA clear, quotable sentence answering the query near the top of the page
Existing strong rankingsAI Overviews draw heavily from pages already ranking on page one
Structured dataSchema helps the system parse your content's meaning correctly
FreshnessRecently updated content is favored for queries where currency matters

What it means for your content strategy

Pages that used to earn clicks purely by ranking #1 may now lose some of that traffic to a zero-click AI Overview citing them. The response isn't to fight the feature — it's to make sure that when it does cite you, it cites you accurately and links back, which still delivers brand visibility and qualified traffic even without a click.

How to track it

Standard rank trackers don't show AI Overview appearances. You need to manually check target queries or run a structured prompt panel — the same method behind our GEO practice's monthly visibility scoring.

Don't chase AI Overviews at the expense of rankings. The same content that ranks well is the raw material AI Overviews pull from — fix rankings first, citations follow.
