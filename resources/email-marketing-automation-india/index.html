Email marketing automation for Indian businesses

By Reckona AIUpdated 28 July 20268 min read

Email gets treated as the boring, legacy channel next to WhatsApp and social — but for the right use cases, it still converts reliably and costs almost nothing per send. Here's where automated email actually earns its place.

The flows worth building first

FlowWhy it pays back
Welcome sequenceHighest open rates of any email you'll send — sets expectations and drives the first action
Abandoned cart / incomplete enquiryRecovers revenue that's already 80% there
Post-purchase / post-service follow-upReviews, upsells, and repeat-purchase nudges on autopilot
Re-engagement for dormant contactsCheap to run, recovers a portion of an otherwise wasted list

Deliverability basics most businesses skip

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up correctly — without these, a growing share of your email lands in spam regardless of content quality.
  • List hygiene. Remove hard bounces and chronic non-openers periodically — a "clean" smaller list outperforms a bloated one on deliverability.
  • Consistent sending volume rather than sporadic bulk sends, which trigger spam filters more easily.

Why email still works

Unlike social reach, an email list is an owned channel — no algorithm decides whether your message gets seen. For B2B specifically, email remains one of the highest-ROI channels because decision-makers still process email deliberately, unlike the scroll-past nature of social feeds.
