Business process automation in India: what's actually worth automating

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

Business process automation (BPA) predates the current AI wave by decades — but AI has made it dramatically more capable. Here's what's genuinely worth automating in an Indian company today, and what still needs a human.

BPA vs AI automation: not quite the same thing

Traditional BPA / RPAAI-powered automation
HandlesRigid, rule-based, repetitive stepsJudgment-adjacent tasks with variation — reading, extracting, drafting
Breaks whenInput format changes even slightlyMore resilient to variation, but not infallible
ExampleCopying data between two systems on a scheduleReading a scanned invoice and extracting the right fields regardless of layout

See our fuller breakdown in RPA vs AI automation for exactly where the line sits.

What's actually worth automating first

  • Document generation — certificates, quotes, invoices from structured data.
  • Data entry between systems — the classic BPA win, still valuable.
  • Report compilation — daily/weekly reports assembled from existing data sources.
  • Approval workflows — routing requests through the right people automatically.

Common pitfalls in Indian implementations

  • Automating a broken process. If the underlying process is inconsistent, automation just breaks faster and more often.
  • No exception handling. A workflow that fails silently on edge cases erodes trust quickly.
  • Underestimating change management. Staff need to trust and adopt the new workflow, not route around it.
