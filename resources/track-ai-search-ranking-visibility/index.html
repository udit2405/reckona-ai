How to track your AI search ranking and visibility

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20267 min read

There's no rank #1 in an AI answer — you're either mentioned or you're not, described accurately or not. Traditional rank trackers can't measure this at all. Here's the method that actually works.

Why rank trackers don't work here

A rank tracker checks your position on a results page for a fixed keyword. AI answers are generated fresh each time, synthesized from multiple sources, and phrased differently query to query — there's no stable "position" to track. You need a different measurement approach entirely.

The prompt-panel method

StepWhat it involves
1. Build a query panel30–40 real questions your buyers would actually ask, covering your category and comparisons
2. Run it across enginesChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity — each has different training and browsing behavior
3. Score each responseMentioned? Recommended? Described accurately? Competitor comparison included?
4. Track monthlyThe same panel, re-run, to see share-of-voice move over time

Free vs proper tracking

You can run this manually for free — open each engine, type your panel of questions, log the results in a spreadsheet. It works, but it's slow and inconsistent between sessions. A proper program automates the panel, standardizes scoring and benchmarks against named competitors, which is where a monthly GEO audit earns its keep.

Start free, upgrade when it matters. A manual quarterly check is enough to know if you have a problem. A monthly automated panel is what you need once AI visibility is a real revenue channel.
