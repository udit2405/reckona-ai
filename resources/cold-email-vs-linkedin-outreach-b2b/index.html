Cold email vs LinkedIn outreach for B2B: which converts better?

By Reckona AIUpdated 29 July 20267 min read

Both are direct outbound tactics competing for the same buyer's attention, but they perform differently depending on how personalized the approach is and who you're actually trying to reach.

The honest comparison

Cold emailLinkedIn outreach
Typical response rateLow at scale, higher with real personalizationOften higher — feels less anonymous than an inbox email
Context availableLimited unless heavily researchedRich — role, company updates, mutual connections visible
Effort per contactScales more easily with toolingHarder to scale without looking automated and spammy
Best forLarge volume, well-segmented listsHigh-value targets warranting a personal approach

Why generic versions of both fail

A templated cold email blasted to a thousand contacts and a copy-pasted LinkedIn connection message sent to five hundred strangers fail for the same reason — no genuine relevance to the specific recipient. The channel matters less than whether the message demonstrates real research into that person's actual situation.

When to combine them

A common effective sequence: a LinkedIn connection request with a genuine, specific note, followed by a cold email referencing that connection if there's no response — using the channel with more context to warm up the one that scales better. Neither works as well in isolation as this kind of coordinated sequence.

The real lever isn't the channel — it's specificity. A message referencing something genuinely specific to that person or company outperforms a well-designed template on either channel, every time.
