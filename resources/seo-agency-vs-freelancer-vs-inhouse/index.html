SEO agency vs freelancer vs in-house: which fits your stage?

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

There's no universally right answer — the correct choice depends on your budget, how mature your SEO already is, and how much breadth of expertise a single workflow needs. Here's an honest comparison.

Side by side

FreelancerAgencyIn-house
CostLowestMid to highHighest (salary + tools + management)
BreadthUsually one specialtyTechnical, content, links, GEO — full stackDepends entirely on who you hire
Speed to startFastFastSlow — hiring takes months
Institutional knowledgeLimited unless retained long-termBuilds over the engagementDeepest, compounds over years

Which fits which stage

  • Pre-revenue / very small budget: a focused freelancer for a narrow, well-defined task.
  • Growing SME, need multiple disciplines: an agency — technical, content and links run as one coordinated program.
  • Large, mature company: in-house specialists, sometimes supplemented by an agency for surge capacity or specialized skills like GEO.

The hybrid that works well: in-house owns the strategy and institutional knowledge; an agency or specialist fills skill gaps (like GEO or technical audits) that don't justify a full-time hire yet.
