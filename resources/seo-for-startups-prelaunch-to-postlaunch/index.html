SEO for startups: a pre-launch to post-launch checklist

By Reckona AIUpdated 29 July 20268 min read

Most startups treat SEO as a post-launch afterthought, once there's finally time. That delay costs real months of compounding — a few foundational decisions made before launch save significant rework later.

Before launch

  • Domain and URL structure decided correctly — changing this after launch means redirects and lost equity.
  • Technical foundations built in — clean crawlability, mobile performance, schema — see our technical SEO checklist.
  • Google Search Console and Analytics connected from day one, not added months later with no historical data.
  • Positioning clarified — SEO content built on unclear positioning has to be redone once messaging solidifies.

The first 90 days post-launch

PriorityWhy first
Category and comparison contentCaptures buyers actively researching options in your space
Entity and schema setupEstablishes machine-legible facts before you need AI/GEO visibility
Founder LinkedIn presenceBuilds distribution while content is still building organic traction — see founder distribution

Mistakes that cost startups their early foundation

  • Changing URL structure post-launch without proper redirects, losing whatever early equity existed.
  • Publishing content before positioning is settled, then needing to rewrite everything after a pivot.
  • Ignoring GEO entirely at this stage. Category-defining language set early is what AI engines learn to associate with you — see our startup brand and site guide.

SEO compounds from day one, or it doesn't compound at all. A startup that starts SEO foundations at launch has a meaningful head start over one that starts a year later — the gap doesn't close on its own.
