How to choose a digital marketing agency in India

By Reckona AIUpdated 27 July 20268 min read

"Digital marketing agency" covers everything from a single freelancer running Instagram to a 200-person full-service shop. Before comparing agencies, you need to know what kind of partner your business actually needs.

First, decide the scope you need

SituationWhat fits
One clear problem (e.g. no website)A specialist for that single channel
Fragmented marketing across freelancersA full-stack agency running everything as one accountable system
Need AI automation alongside marketingAn AI-first agency that bundles growth and automation, not two separate vendors

What a real proposal should include

  • A specific number. Revenue to gain or cost to remove — not just "we'll improve your marketing."
  • Fixed scope in writing. Exactly what ships, by when, at what cost — before work begins.
  • Named ownership. Who on the team is accountable for what, not an anonymous "our team."
  • A first-90-days plan. What ships in the first sprint, concretely.

Questions worth asking directly

  • "Who owns our ad accounts and analytics — us or you?" (Should always be you.)
  • "What did a similar client's first 90 days actually look like?"
  • "How often do we see working output, not just a status deck?"

Fragmentation is the real enemy. A freelancer for the site, another for ads, an intern on social — the coordination cost alone often outweighs any per-channel savings. One accountable pod against one number usually outperforms five disconnected vendors.
