Landing page optimization checklist: what actually lifts conversion

By Reckona AIUpdated 28 July 20268 min read

Most landing page 'optimization' is cosmetic — a new button color, a reworded headline — while the structural issues that actually kill conversion go untouched. Here's the checklist in the order that matters.

Fix these before anything cosmetic

ElementWhat kills conversion
Above-the-fold clarityA visitor who can't tell what you offer in 5 seconds leaves — the single biggest lever
Form lengthEvery extra field measurably drops completion — ask only what you need for the first touch
Page speedSlow load on mobile loses visitors before they see the offer — see our speed and conversion guide
Single, clear call to actionCompeting CTAs split attention and reduce clicks on both

Trust signals that actually move the needle

  • Specific, verifiable proof — a real number or named result, not a vague claim
  • Recognizable client logos or genuine testimonials, not stock quotes
  • Clear next-step expectations ("we reply within 4 hours") that reduce the risk of asking

What to test, in order

  1. Headline and above-the-fold offer — the highest-leverage test by far.
  2. Form length and fields — fewer fields almost always wins, but test what you actually need.
  3. CTA copy and placement — specific ("Get my free audit") usually beats generic ("Submit").
  4. Social proof placement — near the CTA, not buried at the bottom.

Don't test five things at once. Change one structural element, measure for a real sample size, then move to the next — simultaneous changes make it impossible to know what actually worked.
