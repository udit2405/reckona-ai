How often should you post on social media?

By Reckona AIUpdated 29 July 20267 min read

There's no universal magic number, but there are realistic benchmarks by platform — and the more important truth is that a schedule you can actually sustain beats an ambitious one you abandon after three weeks.

Benchmark frequency by platform

PlatformRealistic frequency
Instagram (feed + Reels combined)4–7 posts/week
LinkedIn (company + founder combined)3–5 posts/week
YouTube2–4 videos/month, Shorts more frequently
X/ThreadsDaily or near-daily, if maintained at all

Why consistency beats volume

Algorithms across every major platform reward accounts that post predictably over accounts that post sporadically at high volume then go quiet. A steady 3-posts-a-week account typically outperforms one that posts 15 times in a burst and then disappears for a month — both for reach and for audience trust.

How to sustain a schedule without burning out

  • Batch content production. Shoot or write a week's worth in one sitting rather than scrambling daily.
  • Repurpose aggressively. One solid piece of content becomes several posts — see our content repurposing framework.
  • Pick fewer platforms, done well. One channel run consistently beats four run inconsistently — see our social media agency guide.

If you can't sustain the "ideal" frequency, lower it. A realistic 3-posts-a-week schedule maintained for a year outperforms an ambitious daily schedule abandoned after a month, every time.
