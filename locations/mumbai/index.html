Why Mumbai businesses are behind on AI search — even the good ones

Mumbai is India's densest, most competitive market for almost every category Reckona serves — BFSI, D2C and retail, manufacturing and trading houses headquartered here but operating pan-India, media and entertainment, professional services. That density used to mean visibility was a Google Ads and SEO fight. It's now also an AI-search fight: when a Mumbai buyer asks ChatGPT or Gemini "best [category] in Mumbai," most locally dominant brands — the ones who've spent a decade winning the Google fight — aren't showing up at all, because AI visibility and search-engine rankings are measured completely differently.

That gap is wider in Mumbai than in most Indian cities precisely because competition for traditional SEO is so fierce here that everyone's spent years optimizing for the wrong finish line.
