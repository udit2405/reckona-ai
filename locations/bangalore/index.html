Why Bangalore is a different sale than everywhere else

Bangalore's buyers — founders, product leads, growth teams — are the most AI-literate audience in India. That cuts two ways. Generic "AI-powered" marketing language that works on a first-time buyer in a smaller city gets seen through immediately here. But it also means Bangalore is the easiest market to win with real technical proof instead of buzzwords: an actual accuracy number, an actual automation pipeline, an actual explanation of how a knowledge assistant is built — not a deck.

There's also a real gap most Bangalore companies haven't closed yet: heavy investment in product-led SEO and content, almost none in AI-search visibility specifically. A SaaS company that's spent years ranking on Google can still be entirely absent when a buyer asks ChatGPT to compare tools in its category — and in a market this crowded with SaaS and startups competing for the same categories, that gap compounds fast.
