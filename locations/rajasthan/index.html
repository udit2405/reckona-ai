Why international buyers are the real AI-search gap here

A jewellery exporter in Jaipur or a textile manufacturer in Bhilwara doesn't just need to rank on Google in India — their real buyers are sourcing managers and retailers abroad, increasingly using ChatGPT and Gemini to shortlist suppliers, verify legitimacy, and compare export capability before ever visiting a trade show or requesting a quote. Most Rajasthan exporters have invested in a trade-directory listing and not much else — which means they're structurally invisible in exactly the research step that now happens first.

This is a different GEO problem than a Mumbai BFSI brand or a Delhi coaching institute faces — it's about being correctly described and cited as a credible, verifiable export partner, not just a locally popular brand.
