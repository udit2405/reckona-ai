Why Delhi NCR's admissions and coaching market needs a different playbook

Delhi NCR runs India's densest coaching, test-prep and admissions market — and also one of the most AI-native buyer bases in the country. Parents and students researching schools, coaching institutes and colleges are already asking ChatGPT and Gemini for shortlists before they ever fill out an enquiry form. Most Delhi NCR education brands are optimized for a Google search that's no longer the only front door.

The admissions funnel itself is also unusually unforgiving here — a slow follow-up during peak admission season loses a family to three other institutes before the counsellor has finished the call. That's a process problem as much as a marketing one, which is why this market specifically rewards Reckona's combination of growth marketing and automation under one retainer, rather than a marketing-only agency.
