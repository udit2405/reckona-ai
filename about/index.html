What we believe

Six rules we actually get held to — not a poster on the wall.

01

Outcomes over output

Every proposal has a number attached — revenue to gain, or cost to remove. If we can't name the number, we don't send the proposal.

02

Ship weekly

Friday demos, every week, no exceptions — working software or a live campaign within 14 days of kickoff, not a deck.

03

Radical clarity

Every quote is fixed and written down before work starts. No hourly billing, no scope creep, no surprise invoice.

04

Build with, not for

You get trained on every system we hand over. If we disappeared tomorrow, your team could still run it.

05

Taste is a moat

The same pod writes the copy, designs the site and reviews the campaign — not three vendors handing off a brief.

06

Honest by default

Every monthly report has a "what didn't work" line next to the wins. We'd rather lose a client than lie to one.
